# Motorbike and Scooter Rentals

Looking for a motorbike rental in Hanoi? Nguyen Tu offers several types of vehicles. The best fit depends on your license eligibility, riding experience, route, rental period, and confirmed availability.

## Standard motorbike: Honda Wave

The Honda Wave is a practical option for customers who prefer this style of motorbike. Indicative daily rental: **150,000 VND**. Confirm the available vehicle and its condition before collection.

## Automatic scooters

**Honda Vision** and **Honda Air Blade** have an indicative daily rental of **200,000 VND** each. **Honda Click / Yamaha Mio** have an indicative daily rental of **150,000 VND**. Ask which specific model is available for your dates.

## 50cc scooters

A 50cc automatic scooter has an indicative daily rental of **200,000 VND**. License requirements can depend on the exact vehicle specifications and current Vietnamese law; do not assume that every visitor may ride without a license.

## Electric motorbikes and electric bicycles

Electric vehicles are also listed among our rental categories. **Rental prices and deposits are quoted individually by vehicle model**; no fixed public price is advertised here.

## Before choosing

Check applicable driving-license rules, helmet requirements, vehicle documents, the rental agreement, deposits and return conditions. If you are unsure which vehicle is suitable, contact the business before booking.
