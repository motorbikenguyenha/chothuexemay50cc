# About Nguyen Tu

**Nguyen Tu Motorbike Rental** (Cho Thuê Xe Máy Nguyễn Tú) provides motorbike and scooter rental options in Hanoi, Vietnam. Our listed vehicle categories include standard motorbikes, automatic scooters, 50cc scooters, electric motorbikes, and electric bicycles.

## How we approach rentals

Before confirming a rental, customers should clarify the available vehicle, rental duration, quoted price, deposit amount, vehicle condition, and any delivery or return arrangements. The rental contract sets out the agreed terms.

We publish indicative prices to help customers compare options. Final prices and availability should always be confirmed with the business before booking.
