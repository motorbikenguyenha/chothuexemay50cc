## How the deposit works

For listed motorbikes and scooters, the deposit generally ranges from **2,000,000 to 5,000,000 VND**, depending on the vehicle. Any alternative document-related security arrangement is subject to individual discussion and a lawful agreement.

Deposits are returned under the terms of the signed rental agreement after the customer returns the vehicle and fulfils the relevant obligations. **Do not assume rentals are available without a deposit.**

## What to confirm before booking

Confirm the exact vehicle and rental period, final rental fee, applicable deposit, condition of the vehicle, handover and return arrangements, and any associated charges.
