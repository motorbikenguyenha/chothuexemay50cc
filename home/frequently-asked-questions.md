# Frequently Asked Questions

## How much does it cost to rent a motorbike in Hanoi?

Nguyen Tu's indicative daily rates start at **150,000 VND** for selected standard motorbikes and scooters. Honda Vision and Honda Air Blade are listed at **200,000 VND per day**. Confirm the final quote before booking.

## Is there a security deposit?

Yes. For listed vehicles, indicative deposits range from **2,000,000 to 5,000,000 VND** depending on the type and model. The confirmed amount must be recorded in the rental agreement.

## Can I rent a scooter for a week or a month?

Weekly and monthly reference prices are available for several models. Some vehicles, including 50cc scooters and electric options, require an individual quote for longer periods.

## Do you rent 50cc scooters?

50cc automatic scooters are listed at **200,000 VND per day** with an indicative **4,000,000 VND** deposit. Confirm the available model and applicable license rules before riding.

## Are electric motorbikes available?

Electric motorbikes and electric bicycles are included in the listed rental categories. Prices and deposits need to be confirmed for the specific model.

## Do I need a driving license?

Whether a license is required depends on the vehicle and the applicable laws. International visitors should verify their eligibility under current Vietnamese rules and applicable international driving-permit requirements before renting or riding.

## When do you open?

The business's published opening hours are **9:00 AM–9:00 PM every day**. Contact the business to confirm any special arrangements.

## Where is Nguyen Tu located?

**112 Nguyen Van Cu Street, Bo De, Long Bien, Hanoi, Vietnam.**

## How do I confirm availability and book?

Call or message **+84 942 467 674**. Ask for the desired vehicle, dates, exact price, deposit and handover details before agreeing to a rental.
